## 👋 Hi, I'm Ariel

I build engineering teams that ship. For the last decade I've led teams across **e-commerce, marketplaces and payments**, from Córdoba to Málaga, at **IskayPet** (Tiendanimal, Kiwoko, Animalis), **Mercado Libre** and **Olapic**.

I'm a management-first, hands-on leader: I partner with Product on discovery, care deeply about psychological safety, and keep my hands dirty building with **AI coding agents** so my teams can adopt them for real, not as a slide.
